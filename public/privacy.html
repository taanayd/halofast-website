If the waitlist is connected and you submit the form, it is intended to collect your email address and basic submission metadata needed to operate and protect the form. Do not submit health information or other sensitive personal data through this waitlist.

Your email address is intended to be used to send occasional HaloFast launch announcements and product updates you asked to receive, and to manage the waitlist. It is not intended to be sold or used for unrelated advertising.

The website may use a third-party form processor (for example, Formspree) to receive and store submissions. That provider may process information on its own infrastructure and under its own terms. Before enabling the form, identify the provider here, review its privacy and data-processing terms, and document any international transfers or safeguards that apply.

5. Retention and security

Waitlist details should be retained only as long as needed for launch updates or until you ask for deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations. Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect submissions. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your waitlist information, withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis, or unsubscribe from updates. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law, including the GDPR. Contact the site operator using the privacy contact address published above. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. Cookies and analytics

This coming-soon site is designed not to require advertising cookies or analytics for the waitlist. Hosting, security, or the selected form provider may process limited technical data according to their own policies. Update this section if analytics or other tracking is added.

8. Children

The launch waitlist is not directed to children under 16. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact the site operator to request deletion.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as HaloFast’s website and services develop. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
