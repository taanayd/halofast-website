Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Waitlist status: HaloFast is in development. The email signup is not connected yet, so this page is not currently collecting waitlist addresses.

1. Who is responsible?

The HaloFast site operator is responsible for the waitlist. Privacy contact: halofastapp@outlook.com.

2. What information may be collected?

When the waitlist is connected, it will collect the email address you submit and technical submission information handled by the form service. Do not submit health information or other sensitive personal data through this waitlist.

3. Why is it used?

Your email address will be used to manage the waitlist and send occasional HaloFast launch announcements and product updates. It will not be sold or used for unrelated advertising.

4. Service providers

The signup is planned to use Formspree to process submissions and send notification emails. It is not active yet. Before enabling it, the site operator should review Formspree’s privacy and data-processing terms and update this notice to describe the configured setup.

5. Retention and security

Waitlist information will be retained according to the configured form service’s retention settings and only for as long as needed to manage the list and send requested updates. You can request deletion using the privacy contact above. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your waitlist information, withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis, or unsubscribe from updates. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law, including the GDPR. Contact the site operator using the privacy contact address published above. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. Cookies and analytics

This site does not require advertising cookies or analytics for the waitlist. Hosting, security, or the form provider may process limited technical data according to their own policies.

8. Children

The launch waitlist is not directed to children under 16. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact the site operator to request deletion.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as HaloFast’s website and services develop. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
